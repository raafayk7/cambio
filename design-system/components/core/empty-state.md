Nothing here — designed, not blank. Class: **Async/data** (support;
the states table requires having one, so this is the standard one).

## Anatomy

- Centered in the region it fills: a small flat illustration slot (suit
  marks or a lone card back — moodboard language, no mascots), one line of
  copy, optionally one primary action.
- Copy in the register the surface allows: lobby gets flavor lowercase
  ("no tables open"), functional surfaces get sentence case.

## States

- `first-use` — no data has ever existed: onboarding energy + a primary
  action ("Start a table").
- `no-results` — a filter/search matched nothing: "no matches" + how to
  clear. Never shows the first-use action as if data doesn't exist.

## Variants

None.

## Rules

- The two states are distinct by contract (page floor) — a component may
  not collapse them into one message.
